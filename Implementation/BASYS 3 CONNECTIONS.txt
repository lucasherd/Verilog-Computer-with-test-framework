BASYS 3 CONNECTIONS

SWITCHES:
BTNUP: RESET, BTNDOWN: STEP
sw 0-1: mode_sel -> 00=tick, 10=Manual, 01=turbo, 11=pause
sw 3-5: display_mux to seven seg -> 000=pc, 001=reg, 010=mem, 011=IMM, 100=cpu_output, #101=inst
sw 10-15: address


LEDS
0-1: mode_sel
15-10: display_mux -> 000=opcode, 001=address,  010=address, 011=opcode, 100=aluflags
